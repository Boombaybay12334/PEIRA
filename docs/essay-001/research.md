# Essay 001 research file: Leipzig, 1813

> **Status:** candidate research for the author. Every row in this file is `candidate — author must verify`; nothing here is verified.

## Brief

1. How did the 1812 campaign and earlier 1813 battles shape what happened at Leipzig?
2. How did Napoleon hold on for so long despite a cavalry shortage?
3. How did the Coalition coordinate multinational armies?
4. Why was a weakened Napoleon still feared?
5. What alternative strategic outcomes were plausible within the three days, and what constraints made them possible or impossible?

### Timing and date disagreements to investigate

- The fetched WHE account gives approximate times for the 19 October withdrawal, Allied attack, and bridge explosion; these are not yet checked against a primary account.
- The fetched pages agree on 16–19 October, but their casualty totals differ. Treat the agreement on dates separately from the disagreement on figures.
- The project brief calls the battle “three days,” while the fetched sources describe 16–19 October as four calendar days; the author should decide whether “three days of main fighting” is the intended wording.
