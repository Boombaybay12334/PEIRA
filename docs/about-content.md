
# About page content (written by the owner)

## Who I am

I'm a computer science student driven by curiosity about the world, its history, and the ideas that have shaped humanity. My interests span history, wars, archaeology, paleontology, science, and technology. With a background in machine learning and AI, I enjoy exploring how modern tools can offer new perspectives on old questions. I'm here to learn, investigate, connect ideas, and document the things I find fascinating.

## What PEIRA is

PEIRA is a personal platform for researching and writing about the things I'm drawn to: history, wars, archaeology, paleontology, and the odd corners in between. It sits at the intersection of those subjects and my work in machine learning and AI, which I use as a lens to look at old subjects from a new angle.

It is a place where I think in public, write in my own way, and build things around my writing (maps, visualizations, experiments) that a normal blog can't hold. Every essay links to its sources and to an open repository, states its limitations, and notes corrections when I get something wrong.
